How it works

  • GameBanana mods are downloaded, unpacked and put in worldbox/Mods. wbmm remembers where each came from, so it can update it.
  • Steam Workshop mods are installed by Steam: Subscribe in Steam opens the mod in the Steam client, you click Subscribe, and NeoModLoader loads it from Steam's Workshop folder. Unsubscribe there to remove it.
  • Turning a mod off writes to NeoModLoader's own mod list, the same one its in-game Mods window uses.
  • Turn on Experimental Mode in WorldBox's settings the first time, or mods won't load.

Mods are code that runs on your PC. Install mods from authors you trust. wbmm checks GameBanana's virus scan before installing, but no scan catches everything.
